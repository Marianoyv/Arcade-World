Why Pac-Man belongs in the catalog

Pac-Man is part of Arcade World's Maze collection. It works as a discovery page for players who want a fast overview before opening the cabinet.

As a maze classic, it belongs in the catalog because maze games are about routing, pressure control and knowing when to take risks. The page also gives search engines stable metadata, related game links and a clear path into the playable room.

How to play

  • Plan the next turn before you reach it.
  • Use corners to reset enemy movement.
  • Do not chase every pickup if the route becomes unsafe.
  • Use local import in the desktop/player flow when you have a supported ROM file you are allowed to use.
