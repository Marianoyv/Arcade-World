More than a wall of game cards

The goal is a platform where each game has useful metadata, controls, related titles, creator source information and a reliable path to play. The browser is the discovery layer; the desktop app is intended to become the durable home for local files, saves and longer sessions.

Arcade World does not hide commercial ROM downloads. Public play should come from approved releases, while personal files remain local to the player's device.
