Handheld PCs Are Too Expensive Now

Portable PC gaming is booming, but premium handheld prices and component shortages are pushing players to think harder about value.

By Arcade World Editorial August 23, 2026 1 min read
TL;DR The cool portable PCs are getting pricey. Retro players do not need to spend that much to have a strong setup.
Why gamers care If you just want arcade classics, a browser catalog plus local emulator can beat buying an expensive handheld.
Move Use Arcade World to discover games, then play serious sessions with local import.

The short version

Handheld PCs are sick, but the top models are moving into luxury territory. For a lot of retro players, that is overkill. You do not need a premium portable PC to play Pac-Man, Galaga, NES homebrew or Game Boy releases.

What changed

Better chips, more RAM, bigger screens and storage shortages are pushing prices up. The result is simple: portable PC gaming is more powerful, but less casual.

What to do

Use the web for discovery and a local emulator for clean play. Save favorites, build a list and only buy hardware when you know what you actually play.
