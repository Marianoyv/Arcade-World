Homebrew Creators Are the Clean Path

Creator-approved games, homebrew releases and official links are safer and more scalable than chasing copyrighted ROMs.

By Arcade World Editorial August 23, 2026 1 min read
TL;DR The best trustworthy catalog comes from creators, not hidden ROM dumps.
Why gamers care Players get safer downloads. Creators get credit. The site gets trust.
Move Use official links and submit homebrew games with clear permission.

The short version

A web arcade with traffic should not depend on shady files. It should become useful to people making new retro games.

Why creators matter

Homebrew devs already have official pages, screenshots, updates and communities. Featuring them creates original content instead of copyright risk.

For Arcade World

The clean move is creator pages, official links, attribution and a simple submission path.
